About Me

CS student, builder, and aspiring founder focused on creating products people actually use.

🎓 Education

Computer Science student at Vancouver Island University, I care most about user experience, trust, and long-term retention. I enjoy building systems where small design decisions compound over time, especially in social and AI-driven products.

💻 Expertise

Full-stack web development with React, Next.js, and Firebase, with a focus on integrating AI models like OpenAI GPT and Google Gemini into real products. Comfortable working across the entire stack — from designing responsive frontends to building serverless backends, managing databases, and deploying to production.

🚀 Entrepreneurship

Founder of Looply, an AI-powered web app that helps creators and builders plan content strategies and scope out their product ideas. Instead of generic chatbots, Looply uses structured workflows powered by OpenAI and Gemini to guide users step by step remembering their goals, niche, and progress across sessions. Built from the ground up with React, Firebase, and serverless architecture, and currently preparing for launch.

🌍 Global Access
